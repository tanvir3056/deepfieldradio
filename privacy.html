Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

This policy covers the Deep Field Radio website and Deep Field Radio Uploader, the tool we use to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

Who uses the Uploader

Only Deep Field Radio. The Uploader signs in to a single Google account, the one that owns the Deep Field Radio YouTube channel. It is not a public app and no one else can sign in to it.

What the Uploader accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload Deep Field Radio's finished videos to its own channel, with a title, description and tags we write.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel it is signed in to and to read the status and public statistics of the videos it uploaded.

It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, commenters, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

Storage and sharing

The only stored item is the sign-in token for our own account, kept encrypted (macOS Keychain and GitHub Actions encrypted secrets). Upload records hold our own video IDs and links. We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through Google or YouTube APIs, and we do not use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using the Uploader you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. On revocation, or on request, we delete the stored token within 7 days. Upload records contain no personal data.

The website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers.
